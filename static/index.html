Clinical Reference & Guide

Understanding Liver Function Tests (LFTs) and HepaticShield algorithms.

Understanding Biochemical Indicators

Total Bilirubin

An indicator of biliary clearance. High levels signal liver congestion, biliary obstruction, or chronic hepatitis.

Alkaline Phosphatase (ALP)

Concentrated in biliary tract membranes. Elevated ALP indicates cholestasis, primary biliary cirrhosis, or duct blockages.

ALT & AST (Transaminases)

Intracellular enzymes that leak during cellular damage (hepatocellular necrosis). ALT is highly liver-specific; AST is also found in muscle.

Albumin & A/G Ratio

Reflects synthetic liver capacity. Since albumin is manufactured in hepatocytes, low values and a depressed Albumin/Globulin ratio (<1.0) suggest severe, chronic impairment or cirrhosis.
